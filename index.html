The Studio

Every room is a decision, made slowly.

AUM Design Studio is a Mumbai-based interior design practice led by Omkar Patil and Jaya Ushakoyal, working across residential and commercial spaces with a single throughline: luxury that reads as calm, not spectacle.

The studio carries each project end to end — from first sketch to final handover — so nothing is left to interpretation between the drawing and the room itself.

Detail

Even the smallest fitting is a decision.

A brass pull, chosen and placed the way everything else is — considered on its own, not left to whoever's on site that day. It's the same instinct at every scale, from a door handle to a floor plan.
